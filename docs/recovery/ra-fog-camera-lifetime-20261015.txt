Changes beyond the held proposal
1. Raw-position inversion previously accepted an integer merely because it
round-tripped. Adjacent raw integers may decode identically when a large origin
swallows quantization increments. Reject matching in-range neighbors as well;
positive q makes decoding monotonic. Explicit separate double product/sum avoids
contracting this admission operation. This is still a strict supported subset,
not proof of raw bytes, original packet ownership or PS2 numeric equivalence.
2. exactFloat rejects nonfinite/out-of-float-range doubles BEFORE narrowing.
3. Fog/source/scene/pose comparisons use componentwise bit identity. Include all
pose and CameraSceneState fields and CameraViewOutput metadata, not only position
XYZ and matrices. Never memcmp whole padded structs.
4. Destroy the already-closed Session explicitly before clearing the matching
DisplayInputs fog slot. Do not touch a foreign replacement slot; no clear occurs
unless fogAddress is nonnull. Fog backing remains alive through Session destruction.
5. Both originalSelectedSchedulingJoined and originalFogBit02Published are
immutable false. Numeric distanceCandidate/computeFogCandidate02 remain observations.

Mutation audit
Current Session matrix/pose writes occur in prepare, resetHuman225B0C,
advanceSceneChild, advanceCameraChild, press, and prepareGameplayFogRender.
The held delta invalidates each before work, also fail, sceneBorrow,
applyAuthoredShakeEnter, close and abandonAndCloseSpatial. advanceExit obtains
sceneBorrow; it cannot bypass invalidation. Public view/viewport/pose getters are
const. TimeStats/SpeedBoost/PositionStats friend adapters inspected are readers.
Display/video and ending-clock methods do not directly mutate these matrices.
Only a successful actual prepareGameplayFogRender rebuild publishes a monotonically
revisioned observation. Revision exhaustion fails closed; same-frame re-preparation
invalidates older leases, even if resulting matrix values are unchanged.

Ownership audit
Binding retains shared_ptr world and shared_ptr OwnershipBridge. Private publication
requires same camera Session, generation/publication/frame, retained fog pointer,
and preparation revision. Camera publication checks the real bank lifetime control
block, idle/non-read-blocked bank and actual lighting owner. Explicit world or camera
close makes subsequent reads reject, even if the enclosing shared_ptr lives on.
Aggregate fail/close retires the fog binding first. Camera failure retains its prefix
until real close; failed close does not clear the installed fog pointer. Factory
requires an initially empty trackFog3EC slot, installs its retained RA fog fields,
and uses the existing restoreOriginalRaceFog1A98DC range arithmetic. Non-RA factory
paths are unchanged. New roster and navigation ownership code is preserved.

Remaining blockers / limits
- Genuine static selected191, full visibility/entity membership, original selection
240C00 -> distance26F070 -> draw241280 scheduling and actual fog bit02 publication
are not owned here. NO draw admission follows from the observations or raw operands.
- DisplayInputs and other caller objects remain borrowed: they must outlive the
camera binding, including failed-construction/cleanup prefixes. shared_ptr camera
retention does not turn external application/renderer/bank descriptors into owned
objects. No claim that this patch removes the existing caller lifetime contract.
- Snapshot identity detects current differences, not raw pointer mutation/restoration
(ABA) outside Session. There is no thread-safety or concurrent mutation contract.
- Exact binary32 matrix admission can reject importer-normalized double transforms.
Inverse position admission can reject legitimate originals after rounding. Original
raw stream retention would be a stronger future source of packet operands.
- Strict FP/compiler contraction, FTZ/DAZ and PS2 arithmetic parity remain unverified.
The adjacent check improves source-level rejection; it is not runtime verification.
- Actual scene/viewport preparation is mutated by the explicit unused preparation
API. It is not a pure getter; do not call it from an invented rendering schedule.
- Clipping, interpolation, renderer queues, coverage/MSAA and material policies
remain separate. No material FGE substitute was used and no material policy changed.
