The material-local count is zero until its real Entity count acquisition, one while that canonical child is live and zero after authenticated retirement. Neither WaterObjectData nor raw array backing adds an Entity count, and local validators do not recurse through the combined floor.

Cleanup retains the established nesting:

1. Before Config abort, preflight every live Pool, resource, array and child identity, reference, alias, authority membership and total owned count, plus the exact file-close/pin requirements.
2. Existing Config abort, then World400/light withdrawal.

4. Revoke the material's native views and slot alias, then retire its exact canonical authority entry. A refusal restores only those views, retains child reference/count/storage and its array/resource/Pool, and leaves the already-closed file closed.
5. After successful authority retirement, discharge the child's one native-held reference and Application count once, then free only its backing. A backing-only child has no reference/count or authority to discharge.

7. Existing WaterObjectData alias/reference/backing retirement, Pool Entity/reference/count/backing retirement, then provider and outer-array retirement.
