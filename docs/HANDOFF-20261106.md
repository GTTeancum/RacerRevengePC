## Why this optimization is next

Build66's last recorded interval had 305 simulation steps:

- Scheduled total: 1,576,395,300 ns (~5.17 ms/step).
- Racer physics: 848,543,600 ns (~2.78 ms/step for all eight racers).
- Racer contacts: 363,650,200 ns (~1.19 ms/step).
- Racer children: 295,282,200 ns (~0.97 ms/step).
- Race sampling/presentation: about0.35 ms/step each.

These are nested scopes; do not sum children with their parents. Existing
contact adaptation makes three whole-fleet passes using full Bank::visit:
gather, verify and commit. Each nonzero racer visit copies four 313745-byte
snapshots. Eight racers therefore copy about26.35 MB per contact step, even when
there is no collision. The proposed accessor targets that transport without
changing the actual collision calculations or dropping pair checks.

`observeOriginalAiFleet` and dynamic peer projections already use scalar
Bank reads and are cheap; do not mistake those for the expensive full visits.
Presentation has additional full visits, but contacts are the larger target.
