## Findings

1. **Correct branch and orientation construction.** The directional callers at 1EB328..1EB33C and 1EB4A8..1EB4BC supply `a3=0,t0=1,t1=0`. At 289EF4, `t1=0` selects the general path; 289F90 then bypasses the correction block 289F9C..289FC8. The staged pose helper (`original_ui_user_rotate50kg.cpp:25-42`) normalizes up and look independently, in that order, then forms right = up cross look. It neither replaces look with cross(cross(up,look),up) nor normalizes right. The unchanged rotation helper at lines 69-82 remains explicitly different. As an analytic distinction, up=(0,1,0), look=(1,1,0) retains a skew look and a right vector of length 1/sqrt(2) in the new helper; the old correction would remove the look's Y component. Parallel nonzero inputs may produce zero right, which this branch does not reject or repair.

2. **Local capture and incoming translation.** Capture at lines 43-54 copies local indices 8..10 and 4..6, matching original E0/E4/E8 and D0/D4/D8 loads at 28A538..28A56C. `memcpy` avoids arithmetic or world-cache refresh and preserves the captured XYZ representations. Restore at lines 55-68 copies the incoming node's current local translation, matching 289F3C..289F54; it does not import outgoing position, authored rotation position, or world position. Complete output places right/up/look/translation in the source matrix layout, with basis W=0 and translation W=1, matching 28A014..28A038. Omitting saved vector W is sound for this restricted branch and its Rider-initialized W=1 inputs.

3. **Dirty flags and traversal.** `setLocalMatrix49P` performs the same continuation as 28A03C..28A074: mark bit80 on target and all ancestors, preserving unrelated bits; only if the target is ready, clear bit01 and invalidate its first-child chain. Existing `invalidate` visits next siblings before the current node, clears/descends only from ready nodes, and leaves already-dirty nodes' descendants alone. The raw 289A20 range shows the same sibling-first, ready-gated unrolling. No eager world refresh, child bit80 invention, target-sibling invalidation, or topology change is introduced.

4. **Failure atomicity within the helper.** Graph validation and target bounds precede capture writes. Pose arithmetic uses local temporaries and assigns output only on success. Restore builds a temporary matrix, then uses a setter whose remaining admission checks precede its sole local-matrix write and non-failing flag traversal. Under the documented serial, coherent-graph and disjoint-snapshot contract, rejection leaves snapshot output or represented graph state unchanged as applicable. Diagnostic output is intentionally updated. Capture deliberately accepts opaque/nonfinite XYZ copies; restore applies the explicit stricter finite/nonzero-axis native admission.

5. **Loaded state and source-owner safety.** Scene wrappers (`original_ui_rider_model_scene50kg.cpp:204-216`) reject absent/destroyed models before touching their graphs. Failed reloads remain destroyed; successful loads/reloads provide the graph. The snapshot contains only six floats, with no source graph, asset, material, registry, or renderer ownership. The new functions do not acquire/release owners, unload models, invoke callbacks, or mutate cache membership. Existing retained asset ownership is unchanged.

## Remaining integration obligations

- `loaded()` is not proof of selection, observer attachment, or drained graph/packet borrows. The wrapper header explicitly leaves those prerequisites to the owner. Capture must precede outgoing deselection/unload; restore must follow final incoming SELECT/load/attachment.
- Helper-level failure atomicity is not a transaction across destructive callbacks. Orientation preflight can reject bad captured axes before deselection, but it cannot establish a future incoming graph's position/topology or roll back a failed lifecycle transition.
- Native `sqrt`/binary32 arithmetic, FE_TONEAREST, finite input/length admission, and strict floating-point assumptions are declared restrictions. Physical PS2 reciprocal-square-root and host FTZ/DAZ equivalence remain unclaimed, as the staging report correctly states. Static structural agreement does not establish PS2 numerical equivalence or compile/runtime success. No such execution claim is made here.
- Original 289ECC..289EE8 also performs lazy source-global bookkeeping: it tests the guard at gp-0x73D8, initializes global 0x4433D0 to -1 on first entry, and marks the guard initialized. The staged helper does not represent or introduce that global owner. No subsequent instruction in 289EC0 reads or consumes 0x4433D0. This omission is explicitly an unrepresented bookkeeping/global-consumer boundary, not evidence of full-call equivalence; admission here covers the represented math/graph branch only.
