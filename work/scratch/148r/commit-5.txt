Time Trial solo field: the consumed launch and the race's launch provenance take the 1AD520 solo field (fixes consumed_ta_prefix_rejected on a counted Time Trial; every course and the common route) (compile-checked, not run)

Evidence: c148-ta-tt-restart (wt-148r-4, stock TA Time Trial): "consumed_ta_prefix_rejected
source_pc=0" -> "actual consumed TA race/world constructor prefix rejected".

Cause: the racer manager 1AD520 (1AD5A4..5DC) clears the opponent bits 1125C 7..10
for subtype 3 and races one human. The port runs it at session cold entry
(terrain_interactive_session50du.cpp commitRacerManager1AD520), BEFORE the counted
race constructor (the original runs 1A65C0 first and 1AD520 inside its load). The
consumed launch (ConsumedLaunch::matches / renewForRestart) then compared the Course
receipt (entrants 8) with the profile's solo field (entrants 1) and refused before
any constructor step (pc 0). The same exact comparison follows in every course's
world prefix (original_*_world_cold_prefix50la.h, original_race_world_join50la.h,
common route) against the race's launchSelection_.

Fix (one rule, every course and the common route; no course or mode key):
- original_course_race_targets50kx.h: sameLaunch1AD520(launched, current): the exact
  selection, or for subtype 3 the same selection with the 1AD520 field (opponent
  bits cleared, entrants 1). Used by matches and renewForRestart.
- original_race_constructor50la.h: constructConsumedRaceCold1A65C0 records the
  running selection (after 1AD520, checked by the same rule) as launchSelection_,
  so every later same-profile check and the Results roster's field size read the
  race's own field (1 for a Time Trial, 8 otherwise).
